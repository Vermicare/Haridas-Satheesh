# Status & Evidence Rules

This portfolio uses explicit maturity labels.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Implemented — Professional** | Delivered or operated in a real professional environment. |
| **Implemented — Independent** | Built outside employer delivery with a working artifact, repository or system. |
| **Prototype** | Tangible prototype, technical design, workflow or test build exists. |
| **Research Program** | Structured research with defined evidence synthesis, models, passes or recommendations. |
| **Architecture / Concept** | A developed system design that is not represented as deployed. |
| **In Progress** | Active engineering or research work. |
| **Archived Exploration** | Deliberately paused, rejected or reframed after feasibility or market analysis. |

## Evidence principles

1. Concepts are never described as production systems.
2. Professional work is published only at a safe architecture/outcome level.
3. Employer/client data, source code, credentials and internal documents are excluded.
4. Metrics are published only when already documented and appropriate to disclose.
5. Related concept names are grouped into evolution streams rather than presented as separate shipped products.
6. Failed or rejected ideas remain visible where they demonstrate useful reasoning or market learning.
