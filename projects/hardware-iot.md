# Hardware & IoT

## Raspberry Pi Environmental Sensing Platform
**Status:** Prototype / Hardware Architecture

A reusable experimentation platform built around hardware such as:
- Raspberry Pi 5,
- ESP32-S3,
- CO₂ sensing,
- particulate / environmental sensing,
- thermal imaging,
- breadboarding,
- multimeter and electronic instrumentation,
- digital microscopy.

The objective is not a single sensor demo; it is a modular physical-world sensing and experimentation platform.
