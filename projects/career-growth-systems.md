# Career & Growth Systems

These projects treat professional growth as an operating system rather than a collection of one-off applications.

## Career OS
**Status:** Active System

Structured execution model balancing:
- current-role acceleration,
- targeted international opportunities,
- long-term positioning,
- bounded external-income experiments.

---

## Global Job Search System
**Status:** Active Workflow

Recurring opportunity-management system covering:

- role-fit filtering,
- career-jump criteria,

- recruiter communication,
- follow-up.

## Freelance Opportunity Manager
**Status:** Active Experiment

Designed around **problem discovery** rather than generic service advertising.

Core flow:

**problem discovery → company research → verified contact route → tailored outreach → reply classification → follow-up → qualification → scoped opportunity**
