# Climate-Tech & Personal Cooling

## Adaptive Non-AC Personal Cooling System
**Status:** Prototype / Documented R&D

The project began with a practical question:

> Can a person be cooled effectively without refrigerating an entire room?

The earliest direction explored chilled water circulated through cooling media. The concept then evolved away from a conventional air-conditioner architecture toward **localized thermal comfort**.

### Design themes explored
- micro-hydronic heat exchange,
- directed / laminar airflow,
- PCM thermal buffering,
- humidity-aware operation,
- separation of dry comfort air from moisture / heat-rejection air,
- radiant heat suppression,
- condensation and dew-point protection,
- adaptive sensing and control.

## Dual-Zone Atmospheric Cocoon
**Status:** Concept

Separates:
- the dry comfort stream reaching the user,
- from the moisture / heat extraction path.

This is intended to avoid the humidity penalty of direct evaporative cooling.
