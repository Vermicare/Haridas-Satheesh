## Objective and evidence hierarchy

Find whether either of two **new, adapted** long-only NSE cash-equity intraday policies demonstrates a positive, reproducible net-after-cost advantage over NO_TRADE and the matched passive intraday comparator. This is **not** an exact replication of the original US study: it deliberately removes shorting, volatility-target leverage, and SPY market assumptions and adopts a 1-share/whole-share cash cap. No component is assumed profitable.

*Reference A*: Zarattini, Aziz and Barbon, *Beat the Market: An Effective Intraday Momentum Strategy for S&P500 ETF (SPY)*, SFI 24-97, https://www.sfi.ch/en/publications/n-24-97-beat-the-market-an-effective-intraday-momentum-strategy-for-s-p500-etf-spy and https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4824172 . The paper reports a US-SPY historical outcome, **not** an NSE expectation. The published setup uses a 14-session intraday noise area, 30-minute checks, a VWAP-based exit and volatility-targeted sizing up to 4x leverage.
*Independent replication / adverse evidence*: https://github.com/PazSheimy/spy-intraday-momentum-oos reports a strong in-sample replication but only ~0.39 Sharpe May 2024–March 2026. See also https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy . VWAP entry confirmation and typical-price VWAP convention must be specified explicitly; alternate implementations can materially diverge.
*Reference B*: The VWAP mean-reversion challenger **below is our preregistered exploratory hypothesis**, not a published/proven profitable system. No return is borrowed from the momentum paper or claimed for it.
*Exchange*: NSE normal equities session 09:15–15:30 IST, official https://www.nseindia.in/resources/exchange-communication-holidays . Special days, halts, corporate events and non-standard sessions are exclusion/verification events, never silently filled.

## Market-data and chronology contract (fail closed)

One-minute NSE equity cash bars are stamped at their **opening** minute (09:15 through 15:29 IST; 375 distinct one-minute starts for a standard full session). Full-day valid OHLCV, prior close and a reference of 14 **separately verified full prior exchange sessions** are required. Do not use current-day future bars, same-day look-ahead daily OHLC, tomorrow's data, revised history without versioning, third-party unlicensed archives, or imputed first bars. Match exchange calendar and corporate actions point-in-time; retain provider, symbol/instrument identity and SHA-256 evidence privately. `09:15` is mandatory (main PR #89 corrected earlier acceptance of 09:16-first archives).

**Prior-session determinism (review correction, 2026-11-17):** For each target date, enumerate the immediately preceding 14 **regular NSE exchange sessions** in reverse chronological order from the point-in-time official exchange calendar, then restore chronological order. Weekends and published exchange holidays are not sessions. Never replace a missing, partial, halted, corporate-action-ambiguous, or nonstandard prior session with an older available session: mark the target symbol-day `HALT_UNKNOWN` and exclude it from eligibility, recording the reason and count without revealing prices. The previous close must be the official point-in-time close of the immediately preceding exchange session; it cannot be substituted by the last available data-provider bar. Require all 14 prior sessions to have complete 09:15–15:29 bars, valid positive-volume VWAP inputs, stable point-in-time instrument identity and corporate-action treatment. Exceptional/special sessions require separate rules frozen before holdout, otherwise fail closed. The lookback is exactly 14 exchange sessions, never 14 calendar days or the last 14 conveniently available files.

For bar with timestamp `m`, its close and complete volume are not observable until `m + 1 minute`. At a scheduled evaluation time `t`, calculate the signal only from bars whose ending timestamp is <= `t`; next executable paper fill must be from an **entirely later** eligible bar, nominally earliest minute whose start is >= `t + 1 minute`. This conservatively adds at least one full-minute separation from last data, and never permits same-bar lookahead. Model unknown intra-minute fills/rejections as unknown, not guaranteed fills. All signal and execution clocks in Asia/Kolkata.

VWAP on day d at time t uses only fully observed same-session bars: `sum(((H+L+C)/3) * V) / sum(V)`. Require positive cumulative volume. If actual session tick-VWAP data are not available this is **HLC3-volume-weighted bar approximation**, not certified trade-by-trade VWAP. Pre-open/closing auction is NOT incorporated into regular-session VWAP.

### Candidate M: noise-area intraday momentum (NSE adaptation, long-only)

* Fixed lookback **14 prior complete sessions**. At evaluation time t, `sigma_d,t = mean_{i in prior 14 sessions} abs(close_i,t / open_i,09:15 - 1)`. Prior-day input session must have both 09:15 and the same t-observable close; lag strictly one full session.
* `upper = max(open_d,09:15, previous_session_close) * (1 + sigma_d,t)`.
* `lower = min(open_d,09:15, previous_session_close) * (1 - sigma_d,t)`, recorded as diagnostic only; **no short trade**.
* Decision times (IST): **09:45, 10:15, 10:45, 11:15, 11:45, 12:15, 12:45, 13:15, 13:45, 14:15, 14:45**. A t decision uses prices and VWAP from last completed bar, not a candle starting t.
* Enter at most once per session, only if flat and `last_completed_close > upper` **and** `last_completed_close > VWAP`. No future lookahead, no same-minute fill.
* If long, exit when `last_completed_close <= max(upper, VWAP)` on a later checkpoint, or at the forced close gate below; no same-day reentry. No short, flip or leverage.
* This is an **adaptation**, not a claim of byte-level or performance equivalence to the leveraged SPY code. It deliberately omits 2% target volatility and 4x leverage. No retroactive parameter tuning.

### Candidate R: VWAP-discount mean reversion (independent exploratory comparator)

* At each t, take **14 previous complete sessions'** contemporaneous `abs(close_i,t / VWAP_i,t - 1)` and compute their **median** `delta_t`. Require `delta_t > 0`, all denominator volumes positive. Use only past data.
* Same fixed t decisions as Candidate M. Enter once/day, flat only, when `last_completed_close < VWAP_d,t * (1 - 1.5 * delta_t)`.
* Exit when `last_completed_close >= VWAP_d,t` OR `last_completed_close <= entry_fill_price * (1 - 2.0 * delta_at_entry)`, whichever checkpoint comes first, or forced close. `delta_at_entry` is frozen once entry fill is independently established. If a stop and target are both reachable inside a bar with no quote ordering, **HALT_UNKNOWN**, never assume favorable sequence.
* Multipliers 1.5 and 2.0 are **arbitrary prespecified research hypotheses**, NOT independently optimized or validated. Cannot be selected on eventual holdout.

### Shared cash, exit and risk constraints

* Initial **hypothetical INR 10,000** cash; max single simulated order budget INR 2,500 **including estimated entry charges**; integer whole shares only; no leverage, shorts, derivatives or simultaneous positions; at most one entry and one exit per session.
* **14:55 IST** is the forced-close decision/checkpoint for **all** instruments, independent of Closing Auction Session eligibility. The last fully observed bar at that decision is the 14:54 bar. A hypothetical exit may only use a causally later eligible minute starting **14:56 through 14:59 IST**; require a verified flat position **before 15:00 IST**. This is a deliberately conservative research rule, not an exchange or broker guarantee. No closing-auction execution, same-bar fill, guaranteed liquidity, or automatic 15:00 liquidation may be assumed. Missing/late/partial/uncertain exit is **HALT_UNKNOWN**, not an assumed safe close. Retain full-session 09:15–15:29 data integrity for retrospective source eligibility; do not use post-decision bars for signal construction.
* Loss-stop research thresholds (not profitability claims): stop considering new trades on estimated realized + adverse unrealized day loss >= INR 250 or portfolio peak-to-trough loss >= INR 1,000, or when data, latency, time, exchange, eligibility, fee or capital gates fail. Stop cannot guarantee the actual loss will be limited to those amounts.
* Never send real or paper-broker orders. No order credentials or execution hosts in this research branch. Actual exchange and broker permissions for market data and future paper simulation are distinct external gates.
