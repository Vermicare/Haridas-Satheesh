## Newer 2026 SEBI finding — no education loophole

SEBI [8 May 2026 circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/may-2026/norms-for-sharing-and-usage-of-price-data-for-educational-purposes_101293.html), effective **1 July 2026**, modified education-related price-data sharing/usage lag to **30 days** and gave the National Institute of Securities Markets a special **one-day lag for its simulation lab**. This is explicitly **investor education**, not a blanket permission for our **for-profit automated research** nor a self-service 30-day delayed-feed exemption. It also retains requirements around legal agreements and audit trails. No reinterpretation as authorization to bypass NSE §7.4.

## Cost/benefit of available providers

- **Preferred technical route: Upstox historical V3**, when suitable rights are established; free read-only analytics credential supports the category and provides January-2022-onward one-minute history. A token is *not* a license.
- **Alternative: DhanHQ ₹499+GST per month**, official claim up to five years' intraday OHLC; still requires its own account and independently confirmed research/virtual-use rights. No payment or account creation.
- **Alternative: Zerodha Kite Connect ₹500/month**, offers historical and WebSocket data; explicitly prohibits live API data for virtual/mock trading apps. A different broker is not a regulatory workaround.
- **Not useful for current opening-range algorithm:** NSE daily bhavcopy/EOD disclosures lack minute-by-minute price movement. Direct enterprise non-display licensing is economically disproportionate to initial ₹10,000 hypothetical capital.

## What can advance without any provider reply

1. **Run offline synthetic replay and risk invariant CI**, which uses no exchange quote feed; keep all baseline/no-trade comparator, fee shocks and negative results visible. Main currently records 372 synthetic offline tests as of PR #60; count is a software test, not a performance result.
2. **Finalize immutable strategy/holdout manifest and metadata-only data-quality checks** for later use, without ingesting actual NSE candles or using real-time virtual fills.
3. **Permit an independently authorized historical dataset only after inspecting its actual terms**—for example, permitted personal proprietary analytical use, retention, cloud processing, derived stats and whether virtual trading is in or out of scope.
4. **No broad claim that private backtesting is illegal:** the SEBI third-party-sharing circular targets third-party real-time data transfer and virtual-gaming platforms; the NSE §7.4 language is broad enough that we cannot self-exempt QHAP. Only a contract-specific legal determination resolves the ambiguity.
5. **No blanket licence finding by the assistant:** the exact executed account/contract and vendor-to-end-user terms are non-public; changing broker or paying for an API is not proof of approval.
