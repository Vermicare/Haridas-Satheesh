# QHAP-TRADER — synthetic market-event chronology and source-chain integrity (2026-11-12)

**Status: research-only, no authorized market feed or live order route.**
The module [synthetic_feed_chronology.mjs](synthetic_feed_chronology.mjs) is a
pure Node.js, metadata-only laboratory for learning how to reject unsafe
sequences *before* building any user-private feed-adapter. It never reads
market quotes, prices, holdings, order status, authentication, sockets,
broker credentials, or licensed exchange records. The tests create only
handcrafted timestamps and fake SHA-256 strings.

## Why this boundary matters

A successful HTTP response or WebSocket frame does **not** certify a
fresh exchange-level book update, nor does an Upstox timestamp establish a
strict event sequence. The [Upstox Market Data Feed V3 documentation](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-market-data-feed/)
contains a vendor \`currentTs\` timestamp and \`ltpc.ltt\` fields; these are
not independent certified exchange book-update times or a contractual
guarantee of gap-free sequencing. The [Full Market Quotes V3 documentation](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-full-market-quote-v3/)
describes its quote \`timestamp\` as response creation, not proof that depth
changed. Do not substitute a vendor tick timestamp, last trade time,
heartbeat or REST timestamp for validated book update provenance.

The [NSE Data Sharing & Usage Policy](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/nse-data-policy)
states that a relevant agreement defines permitted use, retention, and
redistribution. No code output, declared flag, successful quote request,
GitHub test, or hand-written permission receipt can authorize collection,
storage or paper trading using licensed market data.

## Strict offline fixture boundaries

- \`fixtureKind = HANDCRAFTED_SYNTHETIC_METADATA_ONLY\`; instrument alias
  \`MOCK_EQ_1\` only. Raw instrument keys, account data, quote levels,
  order IDs, access tokens and unexpected vendor response keys are rejected.
- Synthetic event types: \`BOOK_OBSERVATION\`, \`TRADE_OBSERVATION\`,
  \`HEARTBEAT\`, \`DISCONNECT\`, \`RECONNECT\`, \`AUTH_FAILURE\`.
  Each event has a locally assigned ordinal and sanitized mock timestamps,
  its own fake payload hash, and a record hash chained to the previous one.
  This is **not** an exchange-issued sequence ID or cryptographic
  source authentication; SHA-256 without an independent signature or trust
  anchor can be regenerated by an attacker.
- Book observations must show increasing self-declared book timestamps,
  no regressing reported vendor timestamps, no receive-clock regression,
  no duplicate IDs or repeated book digests, and continuity within
  conservative research thresholds. An aged heartbeat cannot refresh
  a stale book. A reconnect or authentication failure always **halts**
  continuity; no automatic resumed trading or inferred fills.
- Bounds are deliberately restrictive: 60 seconds maximum captured
  fixture window; max 2 seconds from declared book metric to local receive
  and to capture end, max 3 seconds from vendor timestamp to receive, 500ms
  maximum future clock skew, 10 seconds between book metrics and between
  adjacent mock windows, 5 seconds maximum age at independent observation.
  These are *engineering test constants*, not guaranteed Upstox/NSE
  performance requirements or proof of a liquid market.
- A clean \`MOCK_CHRONOLOGY_INTERNALLY_CONSISTENT_ONLY\` means **only** that
  the handcrafted fixture has consistent numbering, hashes and clocks.
  \`auditPairedSyntheticFeedWindows\` checks two declared windows and
  rejects duplicate IDs, interval overlaps, unobserved gaps, changed
  instrument aliases and session rollovers. Neither result certifies real
  feed continuity, data rights, permissible research, genuine event
  provenance, a completed market session or ability to model executable
  prices.

## Future *private* adapter requirements, not instructions to enable it

Before anything beyond metadata tests, obtain documented permission for
non-display research, local retention and virtual paper execution from
appropriate provider/account agreement; check market calendar and
instrument-specific sessions; keep any authorized raw frames and identifiers
**off public GitHub**; and independently validate capture clock,
feed-origin authentication, message ordering, disconnection/recovery,
unresolved gaps and book updates. Even a valid user-local stream cannot
establish net profit, fill quality or real-order authorization.

The prototype's current forward protocol still has zero independently
verified market sessions. Previously inspected February 2021 diagnostics
are development evidence, not untouched validation. At least 60 complete,
authorized forward sessions and independent review remain future gates,
not something synthetic traces can fulfill.

    node --test projects/qhap-trader/live_readiness/test_*.mjs

variables blank and asserts that \`realOrderSubmissionPossible\` remains
