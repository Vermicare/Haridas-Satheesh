# Venture & Product Explorations

A serious innovation portfolio should include ideas that were challenged and rejected, not only the ones that survived.

## Enterprise Decision & Action Intelligence Platform
**Status:** Archived Exploration / Market Study

Initial thesis:

**observe → understand → predict → recommend → act → measure → learn**

across enterprise operations.

Market research showed that the broad category is already heavily served by workflow, decision-intelligence, process-intelligence and autonomous-enterprise platforms.

**Decision:** do not pursue as a generic horizontal product.

---

## AI-native Enterprise Operations Intelligence
**Status:** Archived / Reframed

Explored as a system that could identify operational problems, recommend interventions, trigger approved actions and learn from outcomes.

**Learning:** valuable architecture, insufficient differentiation as a broad category.

---

## PMO Portfolio Intelligence Startup Wedge
**Status:** Archived / Reframed

Proposed wedge:
AI for project risks, dependencies, stale governance information, executive reporting and follow-up.

Market scan found substantial existing competition.

**Decision:** do not build a generic AI PMO product without a genuinely underserved problem.

---

## AI Transformation Operating Layer for Microsoft-heavy Mid-market Companies
**Status:** Exploration

Problem hypothesis:

Organizations already using Microsoft 365 / Power Platform often lack a reliable way to determine:
- what to automate,
- what is failing,
- what deserves priority,
- whether improvements create value.

This remains a hypothesis, not a committed product.

---

## Decision Memory / Organizational Memory Study
**Status:** Market Research

Explored a product preserving:

**decision → evidence → rationale → authorization → outcome → learning**

Research found emerging products already targeting this category.

**Learning:** organizational memory alone is not sufficient differentiation.
