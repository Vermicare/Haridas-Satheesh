# Cooling — October 2026 energy-consistency gate

**Stage: Prototype / Documented R&D. Physical comparison measurements: TBD.**

A local-only experimental package (`cooling_energy_consistency_gate_v02_2026-10-21.zip`, not committed) reports a synthetic consistency failure: continuous nonzero reported power alongside an unchanged cumulative energy counter. Such records must be flagged before any efficiency calculation. This is software validation, **not** a measurement of a cooling device.

**Next:** identify instruments, verify calibration/uncertainty, perform a real logging dry run, and conduct repeated ambient → fan-only → prototype comparisons. Record raw time series, power and energy counters, humidity, geometry, interruptions and negative results. **Reframe** if fan-only explains the benefit or energy/condensation constraints erase the advantage.
