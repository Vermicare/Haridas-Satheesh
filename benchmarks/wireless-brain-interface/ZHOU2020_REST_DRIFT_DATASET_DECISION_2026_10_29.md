# WBI — complementary multi-day + instructed-idle dataset decision (2026-10-29)

**Status:** source/experimental-design research only; no dataset downloaded, code run, EEG labels inspected, performance measured, or maturity advanced. **Stage:** Research / Simulation. **Decision:** retain BNCI2014_001 as the frozen nine-subject two-day benchmark; preregister a *separate* Zhou2020 follow-up rather than silently changing the existing test set.

## Why this changes the research plan

The current [BNCI2014_001 benchmark](https://moabb.neurotechx.com/docs/generated/moabb.datasets.BNCI2014_001.html) is nine subjects, two sessions on different days, and four **cued imagery** classes (left hand, right hand, feet, tongue). It is useful for cross-day decoding but its four target classes contain **no separately labeled idle/rest class**. The previously reported A01E 165/288 is a one-subject *confidence-only* cross-day baseline; it cannot establish idle false-action risk or a drift-gate benefit.

[Zhou2020 in MOABB](https://moabb.neurotechx.com/docs/generated/moabb.datasets.Zhou2020.html) instead describes **20 subjects, seven sessions over about two weeks, 500 Hz**, with cue classes **left hand, right hand, feet, instructed idle/rest** (GDF/BioSig event codes 769, 770, 771, 780). The [original 2021 study](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2021.701091) describes six runs of 40 trials per session, including a five-second **instructed idle task**, not naturalistic unprompted inactivity. The MOABB loader's default cohort uses **26 common EEG channels** because the S and A groups have different channel sets; pin loader version, channel selection and preprocessing before results. [MOABB implementation](https://github.com/NeuroTechX/moabb/blob/develop/moabb/datasets/zhou2020.py) and [repackaged public archive](https://zenodo.org/records/18988317) are access/provenance leads, **not verified raw-byte evidence**. Dataset availability, license conditions for each downloaded file, source hashes and loader version remain **TBD** until inspected.

**Crucial limitation:** Zhou2020's cued idle is not a free-living/rest-without-cue condition; a low false-action rate there would **not** establish safe autonomous operation. Zhou2020 has no tongue target, so its four-class accuracy is **not directly comparable** to BNCI2014_001's four-class accuracy. The 2021 study already investigates performance changes across sessions and subjects; do not claim longitudinal MI drift is novel.

## Prospective follow-up — NOT executed or frozen

1. Confirm source provenance, license, full 20-subject session coverage, the two cohort channel configurations, event-code semantics and source-level hashes; retain only privacy-safe aggregate metadata in GitHub. Stop if the source differs materially from the paper or loader.
2. Draft and independently timestamp a **new** Zhou2020 protocol before scoring any held-out sessions. Proposed subject-wise chronological split: sessions 1–3 for training, session 4 for threshold calibration, sessions 5–7 for untouched evaluation. This is a **proposal**, not an implemented or independently sealed split. Missing sessions and label-quality rules must be predeclared.
3. Predeclare whether idle is a fourth class or a separately gated no-action state, with an explicit baseline mapping; do not optimize this choice on held-out labels. Compare ungated, calibrated confidence-only and drift-aware authorization at **identical realized action coverage**, reporting acted/incorrect counts and idle-cue false actions per subject/session. Include an always-abstain control and quantify useful-action retention. Predefine tie handling, threshold grids, uncertainty and failure policy.
4. Freeze model, preprocessing, thresholds and per-session prediction commitments **before** any held-out labels are opened. An ordinary same-process timestamp/hash does **not** prove label blindness; require independently inspectable pre-label evidence or mark independence **TBD**. Do not publish raw EEG or trial-level personal records.
5. Report calibration drift, missingness, artifacts, session effects and negative results. Treat *rest false actions per real minute of unconstrained use*, physical safety, clinical effectiveness and deployment as **TBD**: this cued-task protocol cannot measure them.

## Evidence/advancement decision

**Strongest verified evidence remains** the existing frozen synthetic evaluator and the one-subject A01E confidence-only 165/288 exploratory result. **Largest gap** remains a preregistered, independently sealed, multi-subject matched-coverage evaluation and a separately measured idle false-action rate. **Next experiment:** verify one Zhou2020 subject's *training-session-only* metadata and source hashes, then finalize an independent protocol without reading any test-session labels. **Reframe/stop:** if drift gating cannot beat simple abstention at matched coverage, withdraw drift-safety advantage; if cued idle cannot proxy natural rest, explicitly scope claims to cued idle and seek a different evaluation source.

**Four-lens check:** hiring manager — defensible scope and negative controls; technical reviewer — split/label leakage and channel harmonization remain untested; innovation leader — published prior art precludes novelty claims; founder — no user, deployment, economics or clinical evidence. No new collaboration, release, merge, product claim or external commitment.
