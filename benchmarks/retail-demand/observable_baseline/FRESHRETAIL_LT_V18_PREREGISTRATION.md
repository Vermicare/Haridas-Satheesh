## Decision-changing primary-source discovery

Dingdong-Inc published a *different* public dataset, [FreshRetailNet-LT](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Dingdong-Inc/FreshRetailNet-LT), version 1.0 (dataset card release 2026-02-02), with stock-status annotations, **7,869,549 TRAIN rows and 70,000 EVAL rows**. Its [official leaderboard rules](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Dingdong-Inc/FreshRetailNet-LT-Leaderboard) specify **2025-07-14 through 2025-07-20** as the seven-day forecast window, using historical observations strictly before 2025-07-14. That is a distinct calendar year from the previously inspected **2024-06-26 through 2024-07-02** FreshRetailNet-50K evaluation window. This is a plausible **temporal transfer** dataset, not an independent publisher, blinded benchmark, or proof of data-generating-process independence. Its labels are publicly available, so all interpretation must acknowledge a public-label holdout.

**Pinned publisher revision:** [01c3bb306415e09d3ccd2fe762f33eb3aeb2affd](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Dingdong-Inc/FreshRetailNet-LT/commit/01c3bb306415e09d3ccd2fe762f33eb3aeb2affd) (v1.0).

## Frozen protocol BEFORE any new EVAL labels are inspected

1. **Source gate:** download exact revision to ephemeral private runner storage; stream-hash TRAIN and EVAL bytes against the above pins; reject mismatch, truncation, or same-file paths. The executable `freshretail_lt_source_gate_v18.py` performs this **byte-only** check and does **not** parse evaluation labels. Actual 317 MB acquisition and its results are **TBD**.
2. **TRAIN-only cohort:** from exactly 2025-04-15 through 2025-07-13 (90 calendar days before the forecast origin), require unique (store_id, product_id, dt), complete 90-day training history and valid stock status. Rank eligible series by SHA-256 of `retail-lt-v18-20261028|store_id|product_id`; freeze first **512**. Do not use EVAL presence, stockout status, target, or future completeness to select/replace series. If fewer than 512 eligible or any selected series lacks its seven evaluation dates, **abort and report unavailable**, do not resample or tune.
3. **Forecasts:** preserve exact already-frozen last-observed, seven-day seasonal-naive, causal stockout-history and weekday-median-of-last-four-in-stock-same-weekday logic from v1.2/v1.4. Only adapt input format and the fixed training date window; document any unavoidable incompatibility **before** seeing EVAL labels, or abort. Predictions must be generated and sealed before the EVAL label file is parsed; prohibit EVAL-period `sale_amount`, `hours_sale`, `hours_stock_status`, `stock_hour6_22_cnt` and derivatives as model inputs.
4. **Primary estimand:** weekday median minus last-observed **day-weighted mean absolute error (MAE)** on *observed in-stock* evaluation days, with a **paired whole-store cluster percentile bootstrap of summed absolute errors and corresponding in-stock-day denominators**, 2,000 resamples, seed **20261028**, 95% interval. Preserve every selected product within sampled stores. Superiority gate passes **only if** weekday point MAE < last-observed point MAE **and** the matching interval upper endpoint < 0. A zero-scored cohort or too few independent stores is **inconclusive**, not success.
5. **Secondary descriptive metrics:** same-period seasonal-naive and stockout-history day-weighted MAE; series-macro MAE, WAPE, WPE, counts of stores/products, in-stock/censored/missing days, sensitivity to high-volume stores. No post-hoc winner promotion, unobserved lost-demand accuracy, causal revenue uplift, inventory savings or intervention claim. Opportunity ranking remains **TBD** until an independently justifiable ground-truth decision label exists.
6. **Reporting:** preserve exact source and code hashes, training-only selection digest, Python/pyarrow versions, selected cohort count, all failure logs, prediction-before-label access evidence, per-store paired sums/counts (private runner), aggregate-only public artifacts, bootstrap seed, preregistered decision and clean-runner replication. **No raw publisher rows, employer information or private outreach tracker** in GitHub.

**Risk flags / stop rules:** FreshRetailNet-LT and 50K share a publisher, may have noncomparable normalizations and anonymized identifier mappings, and LT TRAIN appears to span multiple years. Verify no data/identifier overlap and 90-day completeness *before* any efficacy claim. A source-byte match proves identity against the publisher pointer, not publisher authenticity. If the new cohort is missing, schema changes, feature leakage appears, or matching primary interval includes zero, report the negative result and keep maturity unchanged. If stronger models are selected after label inspection, this becomes exploratory, not confirmatory.
